Small towns, serious food

You will not go hungry in the Berg.

Underberg and Himeville punch far above their weight when it comes to food — from farm cafés and wood-fired pizza to proper bistro dinners. Everything below is within easy reach of Oakhaven. Hours and numbers courtesy of the Southern Drakensberg municipality — correct at time of posting, but call ahead in the quieter months.
