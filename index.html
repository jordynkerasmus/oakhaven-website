Adventure on your doorstep

There's always something to do.

From fly fishing at dawn to stargazing after dinner — the Southern Drakensberg doesn't run out of things to offer.

Fly Fishing
2 min from Oakhaven
160 km of rivers and 60 dams. One of South Africa's two premier trout destinations. Day permits from R200/rod.
Hiking
3 km from Oakhaven
Over 70 km of marked trails on the Sani Spoors network — family loops to full-day Drakensberg traverses.
Horse Riding
7 km from Oakhaven
Khotso Horse Trails — 1,300 hectares of foothill country. Summit Eland's Peak at 1,800 m and watch eagles soar below.
Golf
1.5 km from Oakhaven
9-hole, par-72 course at Underberg Country Club. Visitors welcome — weekly competitions on Wednesdays and Saturdays.
River Tubing
7 km from Oakhaven
5 km of the Mzimkulu River through Khotso farm. Exhilarating summer fun for the whole group.
Sani Pass
33 km from Oakhaven
The legendary 4×4 pass to Lesotho — rising to 2,874 m and the highest pub in Africa.

Questions about
Oakhaven.

Everything you need to know before you book — from bedrooms to braais, pets to parking.

Oakhaven sleeps up to 12 guests across 4 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms. The property includes a log cabin and a cottage, both available exclusively to your group for the duration of your stay.

The whole property is yours. No shared spaces, no other guests. The log cabin, cottage, deck, braai area and garden are exclusively yours for the duration of your stay.

Yes — well-behaved pets are welcome. Oakhaven sits on a quiet lane with a garden, so dogs in particular settle in easily. Please let us know when you book so we can make the necessary arrangements.

Sani Pass is 33 km from Oakhaven — about 40 minutes by road. The legendary 4×4 pass climbs to 2,874 m and the highest pub in Africa on the Lesotho border. A day trip from Oakhaven is easy.

Oakhaven is 2 minutes from one of South Africa's two premier trout destinations. The Underberg area has 160 km of rivers and 60 dams. Day permits start from R200 per rod.

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is by 10:00. If you need early check-in or a late check-out, get in touch and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Yes — Oakhaven has backup power so load shedding won't disrupt your stay. The property also has Wi-Fi throughout.
